* A phone field can be marked "It is a mobile" on User fields. In a country whose mobiles carry a digit in front when dialled from anywhere, the number is then stored with it: in Argentina "11 4123 4567" is saved as +54 9 11 4123 4567, and the box shows the 9 as you finish typing. Countries without such a digit store the number as typed, and numbers saved before are left as they are.
