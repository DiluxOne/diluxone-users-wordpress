* An account made by the e-mail link or a social network, or one missing a field that became required later, goes straight to where it was headed after signing in. Asking it for the required fields it has no answer for, and whether that step can be skipped, are DiluxOne Users+ Pro's, shown on User fields › After sign-in as Pro previews.
* Four more settings are DiluxOne Users+ Pro's and show on their screens as Pro previews: letting the second step decide after the e-mail link whether a code proves anything new (the plugin always asks it), which roles may sign in with a social account (every role may), how often a public name can change and keeping it once chosen (it changes whenever its owner likes), and taking the plugin's colours from your theme (it uses its own palette, light and dark).
* A new password signs the account out on every other device, as WordPress does, and Security › Sessions and the account's list of sessions forget those sessions too.
* **Sessions**: how long they last (WordPress's length until you choose one), where they are open and how to close them; a new password signs the account out everywhere else, as WordPress does, and the list forgets those sessions.
